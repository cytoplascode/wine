Getting started

Three things to set up, and only one of them is needed. Tap a step to jump to it.

    Obsidian vault

    Checking…

    Where each bottle lands: one Markdown note, with its photos beside it. Both folders are inside the vault and are created if they don't exist.

    Offline OCR

    Checking…

    Reads the words off a label so the form fills itself in. It runs on this phone — the photo never leaves it — so the model is downloaded once and then works with no connection at all.

    Label finder (optional)

    Checking…

    Adds a Find button to the crop screen. Put the six handles roughly around the label, press it, and a segmentation model traces the label's outline for you.

    The crop screen's Snap already pulls the handles onto the paper's edge, and on our test bottles Find scores about the same — its own case is a label with no visible edge to snap to, such as dark paper on a dark bottle. It costs 38 MB and about a second a photo, which is why it is optional. Everything works without it.
